Repository 1 of 4 뿯½ The Sales Brain

gtm-automation-engine — qualify and write

What it does

A 10-rule contact-qualification engine (no open opp, not an active customer/partner, not in another rep's active sequence, no recent activity, no DNC/opt-out), ZoomInfo ICP list building, account multi-threading, the 5-part cold-email formula, dormant-lead and bounce recovery, MEDDIC discovery, and a pre-call brief generator.

The result

Reps work only contacts that are both legally addressable and commercially valuable, and every email is anchored to a real, current trigger. This is the engine that produces the incremental qualified opportunities.

Owns the headline lever: ~$79K–$119K (illustrative / modeled, not actual results) incremental new-business bookings per rep per year (model on the impact slide).

Repository 2 of 4 뿯½ Live Research

Research Engine — the relevance engine

What it does

An adaptive research engine that pulls public buying signals (press releases, job posts, newsroom pages), survives site redesigns, and exposes its tools directly to the AI agent. Researches many accounts concurrently.

The result

The agent researches 10+ accounts in the time a human checks 2, and every touch references a live event. Relevance is the single biggest driver of reply and meeting rates.

Repository 3 of 4 뿯½ The Runtime

Agent Runtime — scale and compounding

What it does

A durable, self-improving agent. It creates and refines skills from real workflows, remembers every account across sessions, runs scheduled automations unattended, and works with any model provider.

The result

Routine prospecting (morning list-building, lead re-engagement, reporting) runs at 7am with no one touching it. The platform gets measurably better every week, and a top rep's knowledge becomes a shared org skill.

This is the multiplier: it makes the per-rep gain repeatable across every rep and around the clock.

Repository 4 of 4 뿯½ Governance

Governance Layer — protect the number

What it does

Wraps the agent with auto-firing compliance "instincts" (always check ROE / opt-out / ownership), security scanning that blocks data leaks, persistent memory, and verification loops that validate multi-step work.

The result

100% Rules-of-Engagement adherence with zero manual reminders, safe to run on real Salesforce data, and consistent execution so every rep performs closer to the best rep.

Governance Layer does not add gross pipeline. It makes sure the pipeline the platform builds is compliant, safe, and reliably realized.
