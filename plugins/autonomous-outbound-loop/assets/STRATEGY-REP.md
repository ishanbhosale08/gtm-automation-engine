# STRATEGY-REP.md - Rep Playbook

**Author:** ishan bhosale | **Copyright:** 2026 ishan bhosale

## Cadence

- Follow email-sequence-orchestration spacing
- Multi-thread 2-4 contacts; stagger channels
- Log every touch in Salesforce and Outreach same day

## Reply handling

- Positive: handoff briefing to Closer within same tick
- Objection: objection-handler skill
- OOO: reschedule, do not count as negative
- Unsubscribe/DNC: SKIP row and halt sequence for contact

## Human approval

- First touch to net-new enterprise accounts: operator approval recommended
- All sends respect Outlook/Outreach throttling in config
