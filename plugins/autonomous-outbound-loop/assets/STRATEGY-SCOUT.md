## ICP (outbound)

Tune using `workspace/leads/zoominfo-icp-criteria.md` and operator territory.

### Must have

- Business email and persona fit (Finance, Operations, RevOps, E-commerce per product line)
- No Layer 1–3 RoE failures
- Account in operator ROE

### Priority signals

1. Multi-region or multi-system operational complexity
2. New leader in finance or operations role (< 90 days)
3. Hiring for RevOps or operations automation roles
4. Competitor dissatisfaction signals (reviews, job posts, RFP language)

### Disqualifiers

- Fails any RoE layer (do not queue)
- Consumer or micro-SMB below segment threshold

## Handoff to Writer

Include: company summary, 2–4 contacts, signal tier, persona map, Glean internal history summary, WORKABLE verdict.
